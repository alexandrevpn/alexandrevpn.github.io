Welcome to my homepage!

I am an economist at FGV EPGE in Rio de Janeiro. My research is in labor economics, with a focus on informality, bargaining, and labor market power in developing economies, using Brazil as the main empirical setting.

Research interests:

Useful Material

Useful Labor Market Measures

A collection of statistics and indicators useful for empirical research in labor economics, covering market concentration, firm dynamics, job flows, productivity, and market power. Methodological notes and practical examples in R are included.

Numerical Methods (2023)

Materials from the Numerical Methods course I took at FGV EPGE in 2023. The course focused on computational methods for solving structural economic models. Problem sets, , and code implementations are included.
