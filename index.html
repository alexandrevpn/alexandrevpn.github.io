Welcome to my homepage!

I am an economist at FGV EPGE in Rio de Janeiro. My research is in labor economics, with a focus on informality, bargaining, and labor market power in developing economies, using Brazil as the main empirical setting.

Research interests:

  • Macroeconomics

Useful Material

Useful Labor Market Measures

A collection of statistics and indicators useful for empirical research in labor economics, covering market concentration, firm dynamics, job flows, productivity, and market power. Materials include methodological notes and practical examples in R.

Numerical Methods (2023)

Materials from the Numerical Methods course I took at FGV EPGE in 2023. The course focused on computational methods for solving structural economic models. This collection includes problem sets, slides, and code implementations.
